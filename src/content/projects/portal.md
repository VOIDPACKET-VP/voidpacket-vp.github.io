## What it is

Portal connects freelance creative talent — video editors, programmers, animators, and designers — directly with clients looking for project-based work, without the overhead of larger freelance platforms.

## Stack

- **Next.js** for the client-facing marketplace
- **NestJS** API for matching, messaging, and payments logic
- **Supabase** for auth and real-time data

## Status

Core marketplace flow (listing, matching, messaging) is functional; payments integration is next.
